Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wild 6 ("Wild 6," "we," "us," or "our") collects through thewild6.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Site only.

1. Information we collect

The Site itself is a set of static pages. We do not run visitor accounts, and the Site does not include a data-collecting contact form. Information we may receive about you comes from how you choose to reach us:

  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if provided), and the contents of your message.
  • Scheduling a consultation. If you book a call through our Calendly link, Calendly collects the information needed to schedule it (such as your name, email address, and any details you enter in the booking form). That collection is governed by Calendly's own privacy policy, not this one, since Calendly is a separate service we don't control.
  • Basic technical logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, in the ordinary course of serving web pages. We do not add our own analytics or tracking scripts to the Site.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to emails and consultation requests
  • Schedule and prepare for calls you book with us
  • Maintain the basic operation and security of the Site

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies

The Site does not set its own cookies or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers. If you follow a link to a third-party service, such as Calendly, that service may set its own cookies under its own policy.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share, rent, or sell the information described in this policy to third parties, except:

  • With service providers we use to operate our business (such as our email and scheduling providers), to the extent needed for them to provide that service
  • If required to do so by law or a valid legal process

5. Data retention

We keep emails and consultation records for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain our business records, and meet any legal obligations that apply to us.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, by emailing eric.darling@thewild6.com. We will respond to reasonable requests as required by applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to eric.darling@thewild6.com.
