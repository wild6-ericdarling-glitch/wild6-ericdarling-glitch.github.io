What we build inside monday.com

Every project starts with your process, not a template. Below is what each engagement can include. Most clients start with process evaluation, then move into build work.

Process Evaluation

We map how work actually moves through your business today: the tools you use, the handoffs between people, and the steps that slow everyone down. You get a clear picture of where monday.com and automation can remove friction, before we build anything.

  • A working session to map your current process and tools
  • A written summary of bottlenecks and opportunities
  • A recommended monday.com structure before any build work starts

Workflow Automation

We build monday.com automations that handle the repetitive parts of your process: status updates, notifications, approvals, and hand-offs between people, so your team spends less time on manual follow-up.

  • Automations for status changes, notifications, and approvals
  • Recurring task and deadline automation
  • Automations that trigger actions in connected tools

Data & Reporting Solutions

We set up dashboards and reports inside monday.com that show the numbers your team actually needs: pipeline status, workload, and timelines, pulled from your existing boards instead of a separate spreadsheet.

  • Dashboards built from your existing boards
  • Reports for pipeline, workload, and timelines
  • Views tailored to what each team or manager needs to see

Team Collaboration Tools

We configure monday.com boards, forms, and permissions around how your teams actually work together, so requests, updates, and approvals happen in one place instead of scattered across email and chat.

  • Boards and forms designed around how your teams hand off work
  • Permission structures so the right people see the right information
  • Request and approval flows that replace email threads

Software Integrations

We connect monday.com to the other tools your business already runs on: CRMs, inboxes, e-commerce platforms, and finance software, using native integrations and API connections so information doesn't have to be re-entered by hand.

  • Native monday.com integrations with common CRMs and tools
  • API-based connections for platforms without a native integration
  • Two-way syncing so data only has to be entered once

Project Management Setup

We build out monday.com project and task structures for your team: boards, timelines, dependencies, and templates, so new projects start from a working system instead of a blank board.

  • Board and timeline structures for active and upcoming projects
  • Dependency tracking across teams
  • Reusable templates so new projects start ready to go
