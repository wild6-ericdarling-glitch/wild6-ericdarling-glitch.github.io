About Wild 6

Bringing structure to the wild

Wild 6 is a monday.com Silver Partner agency built on a simple idea: tame the chaos, automate the future. We help operations teams turn scattered processes into systems people actually use.

Where the name comes from

Wild 6 is named after founder Eric Darling's six children. The name is a reminder of what the company is actually about: taking something that could easily be chaos and giving it structure, without losing what makes it work.

That same principle carries over into how we approach client operations. Most businesses aren't short on effort, they're short on structure. Our job is to build that structure inside monday.com so the effort your team already puts in goes further.

How we work

We don't start with a template board. We start by understanding how your team actually gets work done today, then build the parts of monday.com, automation, and integration that remove the most friction from that process.
